## Future directions and continuing work

This project is in progress. The remaining work (@sec-remaining) tests whether survival can inform the single-modality loadings without a tuning weight, and introduces modality and survival weights so that the multimodal model chooses its number of programs on real data and the contribution of methylation can be judged. Further extensions include a factor-level shrinkage prior on the number of programs [@BhattacharyaDunson2011; @GriffithsGhahramani2011], joint modelling of several cohorts and modalities, the full 305,352-CpG fit on the computing cluster, a direct comparison with DeSurv, and preparation of the manuscript for journal submission.
