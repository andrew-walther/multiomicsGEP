# Manuscript positioning and provisional journal shortlist

Updated October16,2026. Scope pages checked against official publisher sites.
This is a fit assessment, not a submission decision or acceptance forecast.

The proposed contribution is a Bayesian molecular-program method coupled to
survival, evaluated in simulations and multiple pancreatic-cancer cohorts,
with interpretable expression and methylation structure. The evaluated Single
benchmark still has alpha_F=0; the experimental supervised formulation is not
validated. Do not title/frame the manuscript as established survival-guided
program discovery or improved multimodal prognosis until evidence supports it.

| Candidate | Publisher's scope | Our assessment and evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| **Bioinformatics Advances** | Computational/statistical methods and software, plus biology enabled by advanced computation, focused on molecular/cellular biology. | Provisional first choice if the final contribution is a reproducible computational method with convincing comparative evaluation and a clear PDAC application. Stable fitting, novelty against existing supervised factorization, and defensible program interpretation are needed. |
| **Statistics in Biosciences** | New statistical/quantitative methods, substantive biological motivation and demonstrations; also bioscience practice/case studies. | Strong alternative if inference, prior estimation and algorithm/objective validity become the central methodological contribution. Needs a precise estimand/objective, credible computation, and simulations demonstrating what the method adds. |
| **BMC Bioinformatics** | Computational algorithms, software and statistical models for biological data; assessment emphasizes scientific validity. | A plausible alternative for a well-evaluated, usable method. Its scope does not remove the need for stable fitting, fair comparisons and clear evidence of the method's contribution. |

Sources: [Bioinformatics Advances scope](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformaticsadvances/pages/about),
[Statistics in Biosciences scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/12561/aims-and-scope),
[BMC Bioinformatics scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/12859/aims-and-scope).
The assessment column is our inference from those scopes and this project's
current evidence. No editor has been contacted and no submission made.

## Draft now; distinguish the remaining evidence

1. Draft the biological motivation, observed-data model, actual update equations,
   prediction rule and benchmark design now. State which results belong to the
   evaluated alpha_F0 method versus experimental supervised fitting.
2. Use the frozen external benchmark, completed comparator panel, full-range
   Single feature curve and descriptive CpG inventory as existing evidence.
   Show strong5k→10k program agreement alongside nonuniform whole-range stability.
3. Resolve supervised fitting and rank claims under original gates. Complete
   matched feature/multimodal evaluations before claiming incremental value.
4. Keep expression-derived labels provisional; CpG annotation, enrichment,
   composition/batch explanations and reproducibility determine the biology claim.
5. Agree with advisors on the central claim: a validated computational method
   with PDAC interpretation, or a narrower benchmark/application result. Choose
   the journal after that claim is supported. Check current format/fees and
   institutional funding only once a target is selected; these were not assessed.

Recommendation: use the current full write-up as the manuscript evidence draft,
not as submission-ready text. Discuss Bioinformatics Advances first, with
Statistics in Biosciences as the statistical-methods alternative. Journal
choice cannot compensate for unresolved scientific validation.
