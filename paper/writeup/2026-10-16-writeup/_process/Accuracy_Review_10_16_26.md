## Direct answers to the three questions

### CPTAC measures RNA expression here

Change “Mass-spectrometry proteomics” to **“RNA-seq”** in Table 2 and correct the corresponding Methods paragraph. A consortium name does not determine which assay was used. CPTAC profiled several modalities; this analysis uses its RNA expression matrix.

The local input has 28,057 features, including noncoding transcripts such as A1BG-AS1, with gene-symbol annotations. The related DeSurv manuscript's data methods describe the TCGA/CPTAC training data as transcriptomic. These corroborate Andrew's correction. The earlier internal data audit and source inventory incorrectly classified CPTAC as proteomics; they are the apparent source of the manuscript error and must be corrected as well. The numeric range of an expression matrix alone cannot identify its assay.

Use “RNA-seq expression, supplied on a normalized/log scale” for the role/preprocessing description until exact upstream normalization is documented. Do not infer TPM, counts, or a new transformation from the corrected platform label. In particular, CPTAC's current `platform_log_transform = FALSE` is not evidence that it is proteomics and should not be changed solely to repair the prose.

External provenance: [Cao et al., Proteogenomic Characterization of Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8654574/) describes RNA-seq alongside proteomic and other assays in the CPTAC study. It establishes the study's multiple assays, not the exact upstream processing of this local RDS.

### PACA-AU is the ICGC source, not an additional multimodal cohort

The multimodal loader explicitly reads **TCGA for training and ICGC PACA-AU for validation**. There is no third multimodal cohort. PACA-AU is the Australian pancreatic cancer project within ICGC. See also the [Australian Pancreatic Cancer Genome Initiative's description](https://www.pancreaticcancer.net.au/about-mission/).

The 67-donor sensitivity analysis is a broader population from that same ICGC source. It contains the 50 primary-PDAC donors plus 17 donors with other histologies: 10 invasive IPMN, 4 adenosquamous, 2 acinar, and 1 signet-ring tumor. It is not a new independent validation cohort, and the 50 and 67 must never be added together.

Recommended row labels:

- **ICGC (PACA-AU), primary-PDAC subset** — 50 donors, 30 deaths; primary external validation.
- **Same ICGC cohort, broader histology sensitivity set** — 67 donors, 40 deaths; includes all 50 primary-analysis donors.

There is a legitimate saved sensitivity result behind the row. Retain it with clearer naming or move it to supplementary material; deleting it as a spurious third dataset would obscure an analysis that actually occurred.

### Matched expression, methylation and usable survival

Counts were recomputed directly from the local source objects, with clinical records collapsed to unique ICGC donors and survival required to have a positive observed time and a valid event indicator. No imputation or feature screening was needed to count subjects.

| Population | Both assays before survival exclusions | Usable survival | Deaths | Censored | Role |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| TCGA | 150 patients | **144** | **75** | 69 | Model training |
| ICGC, primary PDAC | 57 donors | **50** | **30** | 20 | Primary validation |
| ICGC, all matched histologies | 79 donors | **67** | **40** | 27 | Broader sensitivity population; includes the 50 above |
| Primary analysis total | 207 | **194** | **105** | 89 | 144 training + 50 validation |
| Broader sensitivity total | 229 | **211** | **115** | 96 | 144 training + 67 validation |

TCGA exclusions: 4 missing survival times and 2 nonpositive times. ICGC exclusions: 11 missing times and 1 nonpositive time; 7 exclusions are among primary-PDAC donors. The ICGC source has 80 RNA clinical rows for 79 donors; repeated RNA samples are collapsed by the multimodal loader. All 144 retained TCGA barcodes represent distinct patient prefixes, and the matched molecular columns agree across assays.

**Interpretation for the advisor:** the primary experiment has 194 evaluable people across development and validation, but only 144 patients/75 deaths estimate the model. The independent primary validation contains only 50 donors/30 deaths. It is reasonable to characterize that validation as limited and the multimodal work as exploratory. Neither the total of 194 nor the seven initialized factors is an adequate stand-alone sample-size justification for learning thousands of loadings, their priors and survival effects. The current C interval, .579–.779, already illustrates substantial uncertainty. These are the available matched patients in the supplied local collection, not an inventory of every participant downloadable from the full consortia. Increasing that inventory would require a separate acquisition/eligibility assessment.

Suggested manuscript text:

> The primary matched-modality analysis included 194 patients with expression, methylation and usable overall-survival data: 144 TCGA patients (75 deaths) for training and 50 ICGC PACA-AU donors with primary PDAC (30 deaths) for external validation. A broader sensitivity analysis used 67 ICGC donors (40 deaths), including the primary-analysis donors and 17 donors with other pancreatic tumor histologies. The training and validation sets were kept separate.

## Results and explanations that checked out

- The five saved single-block C values average **.62668**, correctly rounded to .627. Table4's listed per-series estimates and intervals agree with the saved CSV. This does not resolve donor dependence in pooled inference.
- The single-block simulation contains **540 saved rows**. Aggregating the six displayed scenario/rank combinations reproduces Table3's recovery and activity fractions, including hybrid false-active rates .35 at K6 and .267 at K20.
- The multimodal simulation contains **486 rows,162 per variant**. Re-aggregation reproduces all six selected-framework rows in Table6, including low-noise low-variance recovery1.963/3, correct-sign recall0, and validation C=.6255; all selected-framework fits converge.
- The real-data framework's primary validation C values **.685207 and .675740**, their intervals, and unconverged states agree with the saved sweep summary. The broader-set values reverse their order as reported.
- The feature-count table reproduces the saved four full-training/conditional-CV summaries. It correctly reports no consistent C gain, no new external score, varying agreement, and conditional preprocessing.
- The25k stable-arithmetic result reproduces saved **C=.983854, risk SD39086, logPL−40177 and the500-sweep cap**. The manuscript appropriately refuses to call high training C improved prediction.
- The fixed-q paragraph matches the mechanism record: **10 versus300 iterations, outputs_converged=FALSE, maximum relative change .0191488,67 nonnegative recorded prior steps and three monitor decreases**. It correctly avoids claiming global ascent/adoption.
- The algebraic expected residual and predictor-moment identities in Appendices A/B are correct under their stated mean-field assumptions. Cox score versus diagonal working curvature, plug-in versus expected partial likelihood, and nonadditive modality shares are usefully distinguished. The implementation claim about carried beta priors needs the M1 qualification.
- The distinction between latent activities, prediction projections and biological signature scores is clear. The manuscript correctly discloses alpha_F=0 and does not claim a completed predictive DeSurv head-to-head.
