5. GEP Loading Heatmaps

For each DeSurv-aligned configuration, the heatmap shows posterior mean gene weights \(\hat{F}_{jk}\) for the top 40 genes (by maximum \(|\hat{F}_{jk}|\) across all factors). Rows are genes; columns are factors. Colour encodes the weight (blue = negative / low expression associated with high loading, red = positive).

Asterisked factor labels (*) mark active factors (|β̂| > 0.001). The column width and visual complexity increase with K; for D3/D4 (K=7) and D5 (K=8), ARD prunes most factors to near-zero, leaving a sparse set of biologically relevant programs.
