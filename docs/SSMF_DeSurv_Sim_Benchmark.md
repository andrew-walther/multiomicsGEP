# Comprehensive Benchmarking Plan: SSBMF vs. DeSurv

## 1. Objective and Core Hypotheses
To rigorously evaluate the Supervised Survival Bayesian Matrix Factorization (SSBMF) method against the frequentist DeSurv algorithm using both synthetic data and real-world pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) cohorts. 

**Core Hypotheses to Prove:**
1. **Automated Nature:** SSBMF eliminates the need for heuristic grid searches over the number of factors ($K$) and regularization penalties ($\lambda$, $\xi$) through Automatic Relevance Determination (ARD) and Empirical Bayes Normal Means (EBNM) priors.
2. **Uncertainty Quantification:** SSBMF provides robust posterior credible intervals for patient loadings ($L$), biological factors ($F$), and survival coefficients ($\beta$), unlike the point estimates generated by DeSurv.
3. **Predictive Flexibility:** By optimizing a weighted ELBO, SSBMF achieves equal or superior out-of-sample survival prediction (C-index) and patient stratification compared to DeSurv, without overfitting.

---

## 2. Model Specifications

### A. The Baseline: DeSurv (Frequentist)
* **Objective:** Joint optimization of NMF reconstruction loss and Cox partial log-likelihood.
* **Hyperparameter Burden:** Requires a 4D grid search across $K$ (factors), $\alpha$ (survival weight), $\lambda$ (penalty magnitude), and $\xi$ (L1/L2 balance).
* **Outputs:** Deterministic point estimates for $W, H, \beta$.

### B. The Proposed Method: SSBMF (Bayesian)
* **Objective:** Maximization of the Evidence Lower Bound (ELBO) with a fractional likelihood weight ($\alpha$) balancing the genomics and survival data.
  $$\text{ELBO} = (1-\alpha) E_q[\log P(Y|L,F,\tau)] + \alpha E_q[\log P(t,\delta|L,\beta)] - \text{KL}$$
* **Algorithm:** Factor-wise Coordinate-Ascent Variational Inference (CAVI) sequentially updating $L \rightarrow F \rightarrow \beta \rightarrow \tau$ until convergence.
* **Priors:** Point-exponential (EBNM) for $L$ and $F$ to enforce non-negativity and sparsity; Point-normal/Laplace for $\beta$ to enforce sparsity and act as an ARD mechanism.
* **Hyperparameter Burden:** Requires a 1D grid search over $\alpha$ only. $K_{\max}$ is set high, and ARD prunes inactive factors. Regularization is learned empirically from the data.

---

## 3. The Weighting Parameter ($\alpha$) Integration
A weighting parameter is strictly necessary because the genomics likelihood aggregates over $N \times P$ observations, while the survival likelihood aggregates over only $N$ observations. Without $\alpha$, the transcriptomic volume will wash out the clinical signal. 

In the SSBMF CAVI updates, $\alpha$ scales the expected log-likelihoods, directly modifying the EBNM inputs ($A$ and $B$ coefficients):

* **For Loadings ($q_{l_k}$):**
  $$A_{ik} = (1-\alpha) \sum_j \tau_j \overline{f_{jk}^2} + \alpha W_{ii} \overline{\beta_k^2}$$
  $$B_{ik} = (1-\alpha) \sum_j \tau_j \overline{R_{ij}}^{-k} \overline{f_{jk}} + \alpha W_{ii} \overline{z_i}^{-k} \overline{\beta_k}$$
* **For Factors ($q_{f_k}$):**
  $$A_{jk} = (1-\alpha) \sum_i \tau_j \overline{l_{ik}^2}$$
  $$B_{jk} = (1-\alpha) \sum_i \tau_j \overline{R_{ij}}^{-k} \overline{l_{ik}}$$
* **For Survival Coefficients ($q_{\beta_k}$):**
  $$A_k = \alpha \sum_i W_{ii} \overline{l_{ik}^2}$$
  $$B_k = \alpha \sum_i W_{ii} \overline{z_i}^{-k} \overline{l_{ik}}$$

## 4. Implementation & Operational Guidelines

### A. Initialization and Restarts
Because the ELBO is non-convex, initialization dictates the quality of the final posterior. 
1. **Primary Initialization:** Perform a rank-$K_{\max}$ SVD on the log-transformed genomics matrix $Y$ to initialize $\overline{L}$ and $\overline{F}$. Run a standard Cox PH model on $\overline{L}$ to initialize $\overline{\beta}$.
2. **Robustness:** Run the SSBMF algorithm using 1 SVD-based initialization and 4 random sparse initializations. For each fold/dataset, select the model that yields the highest final ELBO to mitigate local optima traps.

### B. Outer-Loop Cox Approximation (IRLS)
The Cox partial likelihood requires a 2nd-order Taylor approximation. 
* At the start of every outer CAVI iteration $b$, explicitly recompute the linear predictor $\hat{\eta}$, the Cox score $u_i$, and the negative Hessian $W_{ii}$ using the current posterior means. 
* These form the working response $z_i$ and weights $W_{ii}$ passed to the $q_L$ and $q_\beta$ EBNM updates.

### C. Convergence Criteria
The algorithm terminates when the maximum absolute change in the posterior means of $L$ and $\beta$ drops below a strict tolerance threshold ($\epsilon < 1e-4$). Alternatively, track the fractional change in the ELBO (`|ELBO_new - ELBO_old| / |ELBO_old| < 1e-5`).

## 5. Phase 1: Synthetic Data Proof-of-Concept
*Goal: Mathematically validate ARD and Uncertainty Quantification in a controlled environment.*

**Data Generation:**
1. Generate synthetic matrix $Y$ ($N=300$, $P=1000$) and survival times ($t, \delta$ with 30% right-censoring) based on a ground truth of exactly $K_{true} = 5$ active biological programs.
2. **The "Distractor" Constraint:** Only 2 of the 5 programs will have non-zero $\beta$ coefficients (prognostic). The 3 non-prognostic factors will be generated with significantly higher transcriptomic variance (e.g., 5x larger magnitude in $F$) to ensure the model must rely on $\alpha$ to isolate subtle clinical signals from loud transcriptomic noise.

**Evaluation Metrics:**
* **ARD Verification:** Initialize SSBMF with $K_{\max} = 15$. Verify that the expected variance of the 10 inactive factors shrinks exactly to zero across CAVI iterations.
* **Uncertainty Coverage:** Calculate the 95% credible intervals for the estimated $\beta$ parameters. Report the coverage probability (how often the ground truth $\beta$ falls within the credible interval).
* **Coefficient Recovery:** Mean Squared Error (MSE) of estimated $\beta$ vs true $\beta$.

## 6. Phase 2: PDAC Real-World Benchmarking
*Goal: Replicate the DeSurv methodology to provide a direct, apples-to-apples comparison using clinical data.*

### 6.1. Data Setup (Identical to DeSurv)
* **Pre-processing:** Log2+1 transformation, filter to top 2000 highly expressed/variable genes, and rank-transform across samples.
* **Training/Discovery Cohort:** TCGA-PAAD ($n=144$) and CPTAC-PDAC ($n=129$).
* **External Validation Cohorts:** PACA-AU (array & RNA-seq), Dijk, Puleo, and Moffitt.

### 6.2. Training and Hyperparameter Tuning
1. Perform stratified 5-fold cross-validation on the combined TCGA/CPTAC dataset.
2. **For DeSurv:** Run the full grid search ($K \in [2,12]$, $\alpha \in [0.1, 0.9]$, $\lambda \in 10^{\{-3 \dots 3\}}$, $\xi \in [0, 1]$).
3. **For SSBMF:** Run a 1D grid search over $\alpha \in [0.1, 0.9]$, initializing with $K_{\max} = 15$.
4. Select the optimal $\alpha$ for SSBMF based on the maximum cross-validated C-index (1 standard error rule).

### 6.3. External Validation & Clustering
1. Train the final SSBMF model on the full Training Cohort using the optimal $\alpha$.
2. Extract the top 50 factor-specific exemplar genes from the survival-associated factors (where $E[\beta_k] \neq 0$).
3. Project these signatures onto the unseen external validation cohorts.
4. Perform unsupervised consensus clustering on the external cohorts to identify patient subtypes.
